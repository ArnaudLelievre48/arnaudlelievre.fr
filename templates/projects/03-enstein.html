2026-27 Club president · ENSTA Brest

I am president for the year 2026-27 of " ENST’EIN ", a club on ENSTA’s Brest campus where students and visiting alumni share subjects they are passionate about.

I wrote the club’s website that aims to bring presentations and recordings together, helping students revisit past events and explore each topic further.

I gave two presentations already : one on my blackhole simulation, and another on my TIPE which was on using LBM CFD with stochastic diffusion to predict diffusion repartition in river flows. Both of which and available on the website.
